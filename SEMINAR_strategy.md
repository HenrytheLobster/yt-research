# Seminar — Working Strategy Notes

_Living doc. Captures what's decided, what's open, and the offer/upsell thinking._

## Locked (don't relitigate)
- **Audience:** appointment-based health & wellness practices — dental, med spa / aesthetics, chiropractic, physical therapy, optometry, vet. One themed room, vertical-specific ad hooks.
- **Why this cluster:** TAM confirmed via Places API — every vertical has metro depth in the hundreds (1,500+ combined). They share the *same* automation stack (intake → booking → recall → reviews), so one talk serves all of them.
- **Price:** $1,000/seat leaning (credibility + fewer conversions needed: 5–8 seats = the goal). $500 floor. This first one is the **prototype** — reps + a case study, then take it on the road.
- **Venue / timeline:** Alexandria library, ~5 weeks out.
- **Channel:** YouTube ads (Mike's strength), maybe FB. Hyperlocal, pain-first, **never say "AI"** (cold-call learning: owners map "AI" onto legal briefs / horror stories and shut down).
- **Funnel shape:** ad → landing/VSL → seminar signup → back-end done-for-you.
- **Success bar:** 5–10 signups @ $500–1k, ≥2 recurring. Seminar must at least cover ad spend.

## The offer & upsell (the part to think through harder)

**Constraint Mike named:** don't lead with an $18 automation and try to jump them to a $1k seminar — value-whiplash, wrong buyer. The seminar *is* the offer. And building 5–10 custom automations pre-show is too much work + you don't have their data.

**Working resolution:**
- **Front-of-funnel warm-up = FREE, not a paid tripwire.** The lead magnet is the research itself: a free download / mini-training — *"The 5 automations every [dental practice] should steal (and the 3 hyped ones to skip)."* Captures email, proves competence, warms cold traffic to the $1k seat — with none of the $18-tripwire whiplash. The research is the magnet.
- **Intake form lives at the FREE stage,** not as a custom-build promise. It surfaces their pain/needs *and* qualifies *and* warms — and the "custom" deliverable is a **1-page recommendation / roadmap** ("the 3 automations I'd prioritize for your practice"), generated from their answers. Light, no data access, no builds. Demonstrates value pre-show cheaply.
- **Back-end upsell = done-for-you,** built *after* they pay, for *their* specific automation. You never speculatively build 10 — you teach the patterns at the seminar, and build a paying client's one when they sign. That's the recurring revenue.

So: **free research magnet → (optional intake → tailored roadmap) → $1k seminar → done-for-you upsell.** Seminar is the offer; DFY is the upsell; the research feeds all three; nothing gets built speculatively.
